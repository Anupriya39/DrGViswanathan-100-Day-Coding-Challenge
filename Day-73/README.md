## 📌 Problems Solved

### 1. Sign of the Product of an Array

- **LeetCode:** [1822 – Sign of the Product of an Array](https://leetcode.com/problems/sign-of-the-product-of-an-array/)
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Approach:** Count negative numbers and check for zero. The parity of negative numbers determines the sign of the product.
- **Time Complexity:** O(n)
- **Space Complexity:** O(1)

### 2. Partition List

- **LeetCode:** [86 – Partition List](https://leetcode.com/problems/partition-list/)
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Approach:** Used two dummy linked lists to separate nodes smaller than x from nodes greater than or equal to x, preserving their relative order.
- **Time Complexity:** O(n)
- **Space Complexity:** O(1) auxiliary space

## 💡 Key Learnings

- Mathematical observations for array problems.
- Handling zero and negative numbers efficiently.
- Dummy node technique in linked lists.
- Pointer manipulation and maintaining relative order.
- Writing optimized solutions with constant auxiliary space.
